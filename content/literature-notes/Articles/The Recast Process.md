## Highlights
- Phase One: Model Configuration
  Deep-dive on the specifics of your business and how your team operates marketing channels. No two businesses are 
  the same and we will accurately reflect this during model configuration.
  ![](https://getrecast.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/modelconfig-4x.png) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jv5prvbmz6wbdr3aj7fh03k4))
- Phase Two: Model Performance Checks
  We make things hard for our models. A set of rigorous performance checks ensures that they are stable and have identified true causal signal. 
  Said another way, we know that Recast’s models need to be accurate and robust in order for you to make important decisions with them.
  ![](https://getrecast.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/performance-checks-4x.png) ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jv5ps0fw9j7bkgecep34vxxd))
- and Ongoing Calibration
  The work doesn’t stop after model delivery. The out-of-sample forecast accuracy of Recast’s models is automatically validated 
  in-platform every week. You can also work with the Recast team to design lift tests that calibrate the accuracy of your models. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jv5pt8hhwzwp5kgzeweq883g))
