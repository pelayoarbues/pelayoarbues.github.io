## Highlights
- It's no secret that social media has devolved into a [toxic cesspool of disinformation and hate speech](https://futurism.com/the-byte/more-advertisers-to-leave-twitter).
  Without [any meaningful pressure](https://www.cnn.com/2025/01/07/tech/meta-censorship-moderation) to come up with effective guardrails and enforceable policies, social media platforms quickly turned into rage-filled and polarizing echo chambers with one purpose: to keep users hooked on outrage and brain rot so they can display more ads. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e0wvk3t8wghp5n8d7fsr6))
- As detailed in a [yet-to-be-peer-reviewed study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03385), coauthors Petter Törnberg, AI and social media assistant professor, and research assistant Maik Larooij simulated a social media platform that was populated entirely by AI chatbots, powered by OpenAI's GPT-4o large language model, to see if there was anything we could do to stop social media from turning into echo chambers. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e15mxqg09dgfe1b8jcm5v))
- They tested out six specific intervention strategies — including switching to chronological news feeds, boosting diverse viewpoints, hiding social statistics like follower counts, and removing account bios — to stop the platform from turning into a polarized hellscape.
  To their dismay, none of the interventions worked to a satisfactory degree, and only some showed modest effects. Worse yet, as [*Ars Technica* reports](https://arstechnica.com/science/2025/08/study-social-media-probably-cant-be-fixed/), some of them made the situation even worse. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e19yc88eyyh05g7spwmxb))
- For instance, ordering the news feed chronologically reduced attention inequality but floated extreme content to the top.
  It's a sobering reality that flies in the face of companies' promises of constructing a "digital town square" — as billionaire and X owner Elon Musk [once called it](https://futurism.com/twitter-agrees-elon-musk-takeover) — where everybody coexists peacefully. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e1n255yzmezj857g0qcjk))
- With or without intervention, social media platforms may be doomed to devolve into a highly polarized breeding ground for extremist thinking.
  "Can we identify how to improve social media and create online spaces that are actually living up to those early promises of providing a public sphere where we can deliberate and debate politics in a constructive way?" Törnberg asked *Ars*. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e1w8bnge1vd31gbzjps8r))
- The AI and social media assistant professor admitted that using AI isn't a "perfect solution" due to "all kind of biases and limitations." However, the tech can capture "human behavior in a more plausible way."
  Törnberg explained that it's not just triggering pieces of content that result in highly polarized online communities. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k42e222wp4htkvq6f4n7w7cq))
