## Highlights
- Anthropic [publicly filed](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) for an IPO on October 26 and plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker [ANT](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg). The filing showed that Anthropic brought in $16.3 billion in revenue in the first half of 2026, [14x](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) the $1.2 billion it made in the same period of 2025. In addition, Anthropic’s Q2 revenue grew [1,368%](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) year over year while its compute costs grew [300%](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg), implying an improving return on compute spend. Meanwhile, most of the [$80.7 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) first-half net loss it disclosed was a non-cash accounting charge that grew with Anthropic’s valuation, and its operations actually turned a [$1 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) profit in Q2. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c05ac2q3gr5q2m63j2gva2))
- Reactions from the tech world were mostly positive, although skepticism remained. Gavin Baker of Atreides Management [wrote](https://substack.com/redirect/e79814c9-63c3-4841-9a4e-7b9554fca23b?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) on the day of the filing that Anthropic’s revenue had “likely gone vertical in October after they cut token prices.” Box CEO Aaron Levie [noted](https://substack.com/redirect/4178e46b-03fc-4a9c-b985-00f5bcea95f1?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) the S-1’s disclosure that Anthropic’s average Fortune 500 customer spends less than [0.1%](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) of its expenses on Anthropic, arguing that “there’s no reason it can’t be 10-50X higher than this.” Conviction founder Sarah Guo [pointed to](https://substack.com/redirect/e3c35c5b-d0d4-4670-a441-9e9ebb6dadde?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) an “inflection in operating profit” but also a “mismatch [between] consumption based revenue and long term compute obligations.” On the other hand, Gary Marcus, a longtime AI skeptic, [wrote](https://substack.com/redirect/3f9b6040-8459-4ca0-8314-5247a363f401?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) that heavy AI spending by companies including Anthropic has “at least an 80% chance” of being “the greatest capital misallocation in history.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c06ks5fdy3t163xfzz016e))
- Overall, the S-1 seems like a positive update on the health of Anthropic’s business. Anthropic is growing quickly with improving margins, while its customer concentration risk is lessening. Customers outside its top five accounted for [79%](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) of revenue in Q3, up from 64% in Q1. Nevertheless, investors have been concerned about the pace of Anthropic’s growth. At an investor day two weeks before the filing, some were reportedly [disappointed](https://substack.com/redirect/11f9ded3-d4af-4b63-ab4d-2984c1ef4a94?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) that revenue grew 45% from Q2 to Q3 after more than doubling the quarter before, and they discussed a valuation of around $1.5 trillion, down from roughly $2 trillion floated earlier. The S-1 implies Q3 revenue of about [$16.9 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/ef450026-5ed4-4c3e-a5e2-4b267ad0c2b5?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg), consistent with that slowdown, so the case for a higher valuation rests on whether growth picks back up. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c07m5fxrctc7qa4jxykrtp))
- On October 27, Anthropic researchers [published](https://substack.com/redirect/45f3b8a4-94b5-45c7-93fd-df44b7cf1706?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) results from a randomized trial testing whether experts defer to AI agents on judgment calls with no clear answer. The researchers [recruited](https://substack.com/redirect/30a47dae-94f4-4edd-b98b-bd448da47491?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) 61 screened causal-inference practitioners for the study, of whom 50 elected to use Claude Code running Opus 4.8 for a median of 65 minutes while making a series of decisions in a simulated scenario. Each was tasked with [analyzing](https://substack.com/redirect/45f3b8a4-94b5-45c7-93fd-df44b7cf1706?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) data for a fictional farming co-op and deciding whether to expand an advisory program, which the farm’s board would back only if the resulting productivity gain was 3–4%. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c08bfrj16nprrgev7yxh2z))
- The briefing left four analytical choices open: yield per hectare versus per operating cost, difference-in-differences versus a statistical adjustment, enrolled farms versus all members, and per-farm versus production weighting, followed by a final rollout-or-discontinue decision. A coin flip [set](https://substack.com/redirect/30a47dae-94f4-4edd-b98b-bd448da47491?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) the agent’s lean on each decision through a hidden system prompt that told it to argue honestly in favor of the lean direction while still updating its view based on evidence, and to never mislead. Analysts [matched](https://substack.com/redirect/45f3b8a4-94b5-45c7-93fd-df44b7cf1706?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) the agent’s lean 86% of the time, against 50% by chance. In follow-up questions, 77% of participants [echoed](https://substack.com/redirect/45f3b8a4-94b5-45c7-93fd-df44b7cf1706?j=eyJ1IjoiMjIzYTgyIn0.sKvEWKdJVbfjtf9ATUi8jhInkgN0jta26RjThA3a2vg) the agent’s arguments, but only 15% credited the agents for having come up with them. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c0980at73scmehsrz6jmfn))
