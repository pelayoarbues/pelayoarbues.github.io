- ***Introducing the Jitty Price Guide: Your New Best Friend in the Property Market***
  We know buying a home can feel like navigating a maze. The prices, the bidding wars, the stress... it can all get a bit much. That’s where Jitty comes in with the *Price Guide*. Think of it like a handy guide that helps you understand if a property is priced fairly. Using publicly available property data, our Price Guide shows you exactly how the price of a property compares to the average in the local area – all in one easy-to-read visual. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jmfce13vncjrk0pn2adzr6st))
- **What does that mean for you?**
  • You’ll see at a glance if the price per square foot is *below*, *near*, or *above* the local average. No more guessing if you're getting a good deal!
  • This feature is a total game-changer for first-time and second-time buyers, especially those navigating the ever-competitive markets of cities like London.
  *“We want to give homebuyers the tools to make faster, smarter decisions,”* **says Graham Paterson, CEO of Jitty***. “Homebuying is a huge financial and emotional investment, and the more reliable data we can provide, the more confident buyers can feel about their decisions."* ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jmfce55qyj14p0ybq8rmtehx))
- ***Why Does This Matter?***
  Let’s face it – house prices are a big deal, and they can vary wildly from street to street. What if you could *instantly* know whether a property was priced above or below the local market? The Price Guide does exactly that. It's like having your very own personal price checker that helps you make informed choices while saving you time and stress (and possibly even thousands of pounds!).
