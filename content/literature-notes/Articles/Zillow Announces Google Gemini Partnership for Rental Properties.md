## Highlights
- Zillow has partnered with Google’s Gemini AI to enable renters in the United States to find rental properties on a single platform.
  Michael Sherman, SVP Rentals at Zillow, commented, “Starting today, renters can find Zillow listings and book tours directly in Google Gemini. Most renters don’t search in one place. They’re bouncing between apps, tabs, and texts to friends, restarting the process every time they switch. Zillow Rentals is the real estate platform with a connected app inside Gemini. A renter finds a tour time that works, and it gets booked through Zillow, no app switch required. Done.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kx5zyk89gtv92zhv7r7hh9vc))
