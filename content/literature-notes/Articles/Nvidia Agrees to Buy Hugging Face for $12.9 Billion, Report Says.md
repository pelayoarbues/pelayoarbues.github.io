## Highlights
- [Nvidia](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA/) has agreed to buy open-source platform Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, The Information [reported](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-agrees-buy-open-source-model-repository-hugging-face-12-9-billion) on Wednesday, citing a person with knowledge of the deal.
  Deal talks began after Hugging Face, an open-source AI platform developers use to collaborate, test and share tools, received acquisition interest from another suitor, according to The Information. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m11gkecmvp95ajvmq5d71sp9))
- Business Insider [separately reported](https://www.businessinsider.com/hugging-face-could-be-acquired-13-billion-2026-8) that Nvidia had been “in talks” to acquire Hugging Face. The outlet [reported](https://www.businessinsider.com/hugging-face-could-be-acquired-13-billion-2026-8) last week that Hugging Face has been working with a bank to evaluate bidders’ interest in the startup, citing people familiar with the matter.
  CNBC could not independently verify the report. Hugging Face and Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
  If completed, the acquisition would put one of the most widely used platforms for sharing and working with open-source AI models under Nvidia’s ownership, expanding the chipmaker’s reach further into the software and model ecosystem. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m11gkpjvbxsmmqw503k7b94w))
- Nvidia, whose [blockbuster earnings](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html) Wednesday prompted shares to rise 4% in after-hours trading, has made a series of deals in the past year, including a [$20 billion licensing](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/24/nvidia-buying-ai-chip-startup-groq-for-about-20-billion-biggest-deal.html) deal with AI chip startup Groq**.**
  Siddy Jobe, a fund manager at Eonopolis Exponential Technologies funds, said it made sense for Nvidia to target a company like Hugging Face, as Nvidia has made it clear that it is not looking to discriminate between closed and open-source models.

  “It is clear that Nvidia wants to be integrated in the entire stack vertically, going from energy to foundational models and also to applications,” he added. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m11gm0p6ec8t5bbkcmh2k4ka))
