## Highlights
- We introduce Haru-4B, a full-duplex continuous spoken dialogue foundation model capable of listening and speaking simultaneously. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1r10db1c4mjn1xh70d1g2))
- Haru-4B focuses on natural human-AI interaction, learning conversational behaviors such as backchannels, interruptions, and turn-taking from large-scale real-world speech, rather than relying on synthetic conversations or explicitly scripted turn-taking behavior. Our goal is to make interacting with AI feel more like a natural human conversation, unlike interacting with agentic assistants. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1r4mh8vjr90j5dge9xf7x))
- **We are releasing Haru-4B as a pretrained base model**, providing a foundation for further fine-tuning and research on real-time spoken interaction. The model weights are available on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/higurashi-ai/Haru-4B), and the code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/higurashi-ai/Haru). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1r9xg3z0mkzcb72fsndvq))
- Haru-4B at a Glance
  • **Ultra-Low-Latency Interaction:** About 65 ms end-to-end latency on a MacBook.
  • **7.5 Hz Native Full-Duplex Speech-to-Speech:** Simultaneous processing of incoming and outgoing speech every 133 ms through synchronized audio-text streams.
  • **Single-Step Continuous Audio Generation:** A lightweight MLP head with one-step MeanFlow sampling, eliminating the need for an additional acoustic Transformer.
  • **40 Million Hours of Real Speech:** Large-scale multilingual pretraining without incorporating LLM- or TTS-generated turn-based synthetic dialogues.
  • **Multilingual Speech Capabilities:** Training data spanning over 160 languages, with a primary focus on nine languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, and Korean.
  • **Long-Form Full-Duplex Training:** Trained on full-duplex conversations up to 60 minutes long, enabling the model to learn extended conversational dynamics and long-range interaction patterns.
  • **Controllable Character and Voice:** Supports character customization through text prompts and reference audio, enabling control over persona, speaking style, and voice characteristics.
  • **Open Pretrained Base Model:** We are releasing model weights ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/higurashi-ai/Haru-4B)), code ([GitHub](https://github.com/higurashi-ai/Haru)), and demos to support further researc ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1rhvej3k50zmqtqcgbsgb))
- Haru-4B is built on Qwen3.5-4B [1](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:1) and designed to process incoming and outgoing speech simultaneously within a unified language model. Its architecture combines low-rate continuous audio representations, synchronized multi-stream processing, and efficient single-step generation. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1seryz15khfmbdfk30m7r))
- **Native full-duplex multi-stream modeling.** Haru processes three synchronized streams: incoming user audio, outgoing model audio, and model text [2](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:2) [3](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:3). Their embeddings are combined through element-wise addition and processed by a shared language model. This enables simultaneous listening and speaking [4](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:4) without interleaving the three streams token by token [5](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:5) [6](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:6). Compared with interleaving, this reduces the context length to one third while keeping the text stream uninterrupted. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1tg9kydr8b2ax36vmx7ct))
- **Continuous speech tokens at 7.5 Hz.** Most spoken language models rely on discrete audio tokens, often produced through Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ) [7](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:7) [8](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:8), where each audio frame is represented by multiple discrete codebook indices. In contrast, Haru uses 64-dimensional continuous acoustic latents at just 7.5 Hz [9](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:9), representing approximately 133 ms of speech per token. By avoiding discrete codebooks and operating at a low token rate, Haru reduces the number of sequential LLM modeling steps required for training and inference. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1wpfamttp3cjszfsw0cty))
- **Single-step continuous speech generation.** Haru generates continuous acoustic tokens directly from language-model hidden states using a lightweight Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) within a next-latent diffusion framework [10](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:10) [11](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:11) [12](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:12). We primarily train the generation head with Flow Matching [13](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:13), followed by MeanFlow distillation [14](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:14) [15](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:15) for training and inference efficiency. At inference time, each acoustic token is generated in a single sampling step, without iterative flow sampling or an additional acoustic Transformer. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1ww0v73zzxkm2j174ce6n))
- **Chunk-level text alignment.** Instead of aligning text and audio at the individual token level, Haru represents text in chunks [3](https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/#fn:3), followed by padding positions and a dedicated end-of-padding token for predicting speech initiation. This accommodates the different temporal resolutions of text and audio while maintaining synchronization across streams and mitigating catastrophic forgetting of pretrained language-model knowledge. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1wynkxag77e2ykgwkv0qg))
- We believe large-scale real-world speech is essential for learning the dynamics of natural interaction. Unlike synthetic conversations, real recordings contain spontaneous interruptions, hesitations, backchannels, overlapping speech, and irregular turn boundaries. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1x3a45kckvt0jdp43asvt))
- Haru-4B is pretrained using **over 40 million hours of speech**, covering more than 160 languages, with nine primary languages. To learn natural human conversational patterns, we intentionally exclude LLM-generated dialogues and TTS-synthesized speech from our training mixture. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1xb8gnv1dja5hvyq16yb4))
- **Beyond separated speech.** Separated speech makes full-duplex dialogue pretraining scalable, but separation errors and noise can remain, especially in overlapping speech. To further improve natural full-duplex interaction, we are fine-tuning Haru on native full-duplex two-channel conversational recordings. We are also developing full-duplex dialogue and speech generation models to create natural synthetic full-duplex data that enhances response quality, conversational diversity, and character-specific dialogue capabilities. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1ywferxgp3agyx2zztxay))
- **Scaling up to 100B+ parameters.** Although Haru-4B demonstrates efficient full-duplex speech modeling, its relatively small language-model backbone limits its response quality. We are already pretraining larger full-duplex models with over 100 billion parameters, aiming to combine natural conversational timing with stronger language understanding and knowledge. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1zaba9pmbec7hjj7vx8qb))
- **Toward omni-modal full-duplex interaction.** Human interaction involves more than speech. Visual context, facial expressions, and nonverbal cues all contribute to natural conversation. We are extending our architecture to take real-time video input, moving toward omni-modal full-duplex dialogue models that can see, listen, and speak continuously. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7b1zgemj4388e7y3b2qn289))
