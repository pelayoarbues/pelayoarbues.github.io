## Highlights
- **Idol turns one image into a 3D object made of Gaussians.** A photograph, a render or an Iris-3B generation goes in; a splat you can turn around comes out, including the sides the image never showed. It can generate each object at several levels of detail, from 32,768 to 1,048,576 Gaussians. **With 0.66B parameters, it runs in under 8 GB of GPU memory.** ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m6c0brzsh4wk2nb141gs5b2s))
