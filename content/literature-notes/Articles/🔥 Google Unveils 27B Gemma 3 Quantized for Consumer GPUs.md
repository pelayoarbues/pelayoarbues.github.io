## Highlights
- Google released Quantization-Aware Trained (QAT) versions of its Gemma 3 models, including 27B. These models maintain performance while reducing memory needs to run on consumer GPUs. The 27B QAT model loads in 14.1 GB of VRAM. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jsff7a98re53q37mg0xbfwng))
- **Memory Reductions with int4 Quantization**
  QAT lowers memory use for model weights across all Gemma 3 sizes.
  • 27B: from 54 GB (BF16) to 14.1 GB (int4) for model weights 
  • 12B: from 24 GB to 6.6 GB 
  • 4B: from 8 GB to 2.6 GB 
  • 1B: from 2 GB to 0.5 GB ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jsff7dmqfypswnm6vf2fhqz1))
