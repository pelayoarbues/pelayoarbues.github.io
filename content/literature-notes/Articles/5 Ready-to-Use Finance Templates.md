## Highlights
- In finance, this means creating explicit agreements between systems that generate data — like core banking platforms, trading systems, payment processors, and ML scoring engines — and the teams that rely on them for regulatory reporting, risk analytics, reconciliation, and fraud detection. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct5ktjha7sm9tamqp3d2hy))
- Data contracts are machine-readable agreements that define how data should be structured, validated, and governed as it moves between producers and consumers. As data flows through your pipelines, the contract **automatically determines whether the data is fit to use according to agreed specifications**. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct5q4kp1a0wjch79etsp66))
- Instead of detecting problems during month-end close or regulatory submission, teams enforce expectations directly inside data pipelines. Together, domain teams and engineers set expectations about schema, freshness, quality rules, and more, and the contract **makes those expectations testable**. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct5s2fc96s8c74ga6k70sy))
- Not all data contract implementations are equal, though. Some approaches focus on documentation and metadata standards only. Others — like [Soda collaborative contracts](https://soda.io/product/data-contracts?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=data_contracts&utm_content=260309_tom) — enforce validation automatically during ingestion, transformation, or CI/CD workflows. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct5w1vdtmcret0qmyppykd))
- This means you can add automated checks for accuracy, completeness, and timeliness; prevent poor-quality data from reaching downstream systems; and set up proactive alerts to address issues before they escalate. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct5xex63qv88461xp5qwvn))
- When a contract fails, it can:
  • Block pipeline execution (CI/CD gate)
  • Prevent downstream table updates
  • Trigger alerting workflows
  • Mark datasets as invalid in observability tools ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct64jka2z11jvp6hetwy9h))
- In short, for finance, where data errors can trigger regulatory penalties, misstated risk positions, or failed client obligations, enforceable contracts transform reactive firefighting into predictable operations.
  Financial institutions face a unique combination of constraints:
  • Regulatory pressure (BCBS 239, IFRS, SOX, internal audit)
  • Strict reconciliation requirements
  • High financial and reputational risk
  • Complex multi-system architectures
  • Increasing reliance on machine learning outputs ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kkct6h703gfe1w0v0erjzxv0))
