- On Monday, OpenAI and Nvidia jointly [announced](https://openai.com/index/openai-nvidia-systems-partnership/) a letter of intent for a strategic partnership to deploy at least 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems for OpenAI's AI infrastructure, with Nvidia planning to invest up to $100 billion as the systems roll out. OpenAI's planned infrastructure would dwarf existing installations, requiring as much electricity as multiple major cities.
