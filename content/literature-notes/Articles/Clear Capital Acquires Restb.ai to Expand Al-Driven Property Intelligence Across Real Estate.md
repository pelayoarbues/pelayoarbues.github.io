## Highlights
- Clear Capital®, the real estate analytics, data solutions, and valuation technology company, has announced its acquisition of Restb.ai, a Barcelona-based AI-powered computer vision technology for the valuation and real estate industries, to bring deeper property intelligence to real estate decision-making globally.
  This acquisition adds Restb.ai’s image recognition and data enrichment capabilities to Clear Capital’s product suite, which also includes CubiCasa®, the digital floor plan and virtual tour company Clear Capital acquired in 2021. Together, Clear Capital, CubiCasa and Restb.ai will help customers to holistically understand the information that shapes real estate and mortgage decisions, from valuation and floor plans to property condition and characteristics, reducing blind spots in property analysis and supporting more confident decisions.
  Duane Andrews, CEO of Clear Capital, said, “Joining forces with Restb.ai allows us to modernise the valuation landscape. By embedding AI-driven property intelligence into our valuations and mobile floor plan technology, we’re increasing accuracy of decision making for housing finance. These enhancements will bring transparency and data quality to real estate decisions.”
  Xavi Hernando Bonvehi, CEO and Co-Founder of Restb.ai, said, “Clear Capital shares our vision for turning visual property data into trusted, decision-ready intelligence. This empowers Restb.ai with additional resources to accelerate innovation while continuing to support the customers, partners, and brands for real estate.”
  This acquisition builds on Clear Capital’s 2025 strategic investment from GTCR, which supported the company’s plans to expand its technology platform, strengthen its data and valuation offerings, and pursue strategic acquisitions.
