## Highlights
- Palantir is frequently positioned as an AI company competing at the model layer. In reality, it operates at a far more critical level — the decision layer, where intelligence is translated into action.
  The distinction matters. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01knm99mbg8e782sjjsm5pfg98))
- While most companies are focused on generating intelligence through increasingly powerful models, Palantir is building the infrastructure that determines how that intelligence is applied inside real-world systems.
  Through its Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP), Palantir creates a closed-loop system where data, models, and operational workflows are unified into a single execution engine. This system does not merely generate insights — it deploys them.
  The key question is not whether AI will grow. It is whether Palantir becomes the system through which AI is operationalized. If so, it is not just another software company — it becomes a control layer in the AI economy. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01knm99tyd629ram93b2gwgm2c))
