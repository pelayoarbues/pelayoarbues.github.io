Most importantly, the magic behind Labs is what Perplexity is best known for — accurate answers that help you make better decisions. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9hwsmbsjk56kvv3xxhp7k))
- What’s the easiest way to get started? We are constantly updating examples on our [Projects Gallery](https://perplexity.ai/labs). Or, you can pick one thing on your to-do list and try it out. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9j5rm6ct9zx7fqq0vgqww))

From creating a marketing campaign, to analyzing your business finances, to meal planning for the week, Labs is where you turn your best ideas into reality. - Code Generation
  Labs writes and executes code to handle tasks like structuring data, applying formulas, and creating charts, text documents, or spreadsheets. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9jmpg1x6c01795x2vz04b))
- Assets Tab
  All files created during your workflow — from generated charts and images, to CSV and code files — are organized in the “Assets” tab. From there, you can easily view and download everything you need. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9jrwzxxh8gv9wkhb0bwm5))
- Mini Apps
  Labs can develop and deploy simple interactive web apps directly within an “App” tab in your project. This enables the creation of basic dashboards, slideshows, and websites without external development tools. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9jztq3bjbytedmn736bba))
- While Deep Research remains the fastest way to obtain comprehensive answers to in-depth questions— typically within 3 or 4 minutes — Labs is designed to invest more time (10 minutes or longer) and leverage additional tools, such as advanced file generation and mini-app creation. This expanded capability empowers you to develop a broader array of deliverables for your projects. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jwp9k9hmwz8m1vjk6kqyeqxh))
