## Highlights
- Nadella said he is “AGI-pilled,” though not in the usual sense of betting on superintelligence. What matters to him is how the political economy will be organized around a model with “no limits”—one that could become a health care or financial services company, or even “take over the government.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7tk4v0sgr5vjvkjae4s70k8))
- Microsoft is making its case against Anthropic taking over everything. Asked whether Anthropic can still be caught once its models start improving themselves, Nadella said a world that runs on one firm structurally won’t happen because the world will demand more than one firm. He made the same point in his keynote: Intelligence needs to be broadly distributed for multiple firms to thrive, which means there should be “as many models as firms.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7tk4ymxkm2dgxrzx1xe7j93))
- Nadella says he’s trying to convert the model business into a “database business.” Microsoft will let enterprise customers license its [MAI model weights](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-licensed-weights/), run them in their own Azure environments, fine-tune them on proprietary data, and decide when to upgrade. AT&T has already tried it: Its custom MAI model outperformed Sol 6.1 on standard 5G network tasks at one-fiftieth of the cost, according to Microsoft. [Monologue](https://monologue.to/), Every’s dictation app, runs on a similar idea: Its two-person team replaced a roughly 1-trillion-parameter model with one they [fine-tuned themselves](https://every.to/tv?room=classroom&t=7175&utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_fine_tuned_themselves_1), and it’s faster and far cheaper. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7tk5m75wbv5zx4agb146pje))
- s head of evals **[Mike Taylor](https://every.to/@mike_2114?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_mike_taylor_1)** [wrote in August](https://every.to/also-true-for-humans/the-best-ai-agent-builder-is-trapped-inside-microsoft?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_wrote_in_august_1), Claude Code’s bottom-up surge eventually pushed Microsoft to break its exclusivity with OpenAI and bring Claude into Copilot. Now, Microsoft’s latest moves, like the launch of [Autopilot](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/autopilot-meet-your-trusted-personal-teammate/4572611) (Microsoft’s answer to products like [Dots](https://every.to/guides/dots-a-guide-for-putting-openai-s-always-on-agents-to-work?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_dots_1) and [Duet](https://every.to/vibe-check/vibe-check-duet-anthropic-s-always-on-agent?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_duet_1)), suggest the company has to build a better Claude desktop before Claude builds a better Office suite. Copilot used to be a sidebar inside Office. Now the full Office apps run inside Copilot, and [Copilot Workspaces](https://aka.ms/MicrosoftIgnite26Blog) lets people and their agents collaborate using shared context. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7tk5vcg5gqg617z7qqmfg9h))
- Nadella says Microsoft’s ideal team stack now includes full-stack builders, infrastructure people, and scientists, and former team leads are becoming individual contributors who manage agents. (As **[Ryan Sloan](https://every.to/@ryan_17896581442691855_7774e9bcd0c2c10c?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_ryan_sloan_1)** [reported for us in September](https://every.to/p/copilot-gets-a-seat-in-the-org-chart?utm_cta_source=post_4623_post_body_reported_for_us_in_september_1), Microsoft has put Copilot’s agents on the org chart.) Nadella says those contributors will get a token budget, and yes, their pay should go up as an incentive. Most importantly, he stressed that everyone (including him) needs to relearn how work happens from first principles. “We turn our two hundred thousand people into a scale benefit, but it has to transform and work like any AI native startup.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01m7tk65wq97n1wfmdrkxxgfzw))
