## Highlights
- Today marks an exciting milestone in the property world as Jitty, the UK’s first AI-driven property search engine, rolls out its cutting-edge image search feature. 
  Whether you're a serious homebuyer or a property enthusiast who loves browsing beautiful houses, Jitty’s new tool is about to change the way you search for your dream space. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jmfcppnm72tnva1nxs8gtbng))
- **Say Goodbye to Generic Searches – and Hello to Customised Results**
  With Jitty’s new image search, you no longer have to sift through property listings based on standard keywords or vague criteria. Instead, you can simply describe your ideal home in your own words, and Jitty’s AI will do the rest. 
  Our AI interprets images from property listings, identifying key features that matter to you. Whether you're dreaming of a cosy cottage with exposed wooden beams or a sleek modern flat with a standalone bath, Jitty will find the exact match.
  It’s like having a personalised home search assistant in your pocket - one that can spot details like walk-in showers, fireplaces, or a specific exterior style that fits your vision. This goes beyond just looking for a “four-bedroom house”; now, Jitty can find you a home based on its neighbourhood vibe, unique architecture, or even the feeling it gives you. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jmfcpwbxyf4qaxp7nx2es55a))
- **Natural Language Search for Real Estate**
  Imagine being able to search for a property not by numbers, but by what really matters. With Jitty’s natural language search, you can. Rather than inputting a location and toggling through filters, you can simply describe the kind of lifestyle you're after, and Jitty will match you with properties that tick all the boxes. 
  For instance, instead of saying "Three-bed in Brighton," in search filters, you can now search for: "A three-bedroom house with a garden large enough to play football in, within a short commute to London, hardwood floors, and room for a home office."
  This intuitive way of searching makes the whole process faster and much more fun - no more scrolling through irrelevant results. 
  Dubbed the "Instagram for homes" by some users, Jitty is perfect for those who love “roomscrolling” - whether they’re actively house hunting or just keeping an eye on the market. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jmfcq0hqvcp9ddrftek3t2k6))
- **Why Jitty is a Game-Changer**
  Since launching earlier this year, we have already amassed over 75,000 users. The platform doesn't just help people find homes - it enhances the entire browsing experience. 
  Roomscrollers can explore beautifully curated collections of homes, create and share shortlists, collaborate with friends and family, and even leave emojis on properties they like.
  What’s more, Jitty equips users with valuable insights, such as average property prices per square metre in a particular area, helping them make more informed decisions. Whether you're ready to put in an offer or just window shopping, Jitty makes the home search journey both educational and enjoyable.
