## Highlights
- Marc Andreessen, cofounder of the massive venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz — which has its fingers in pretty much every pie in tech *—* has revealed an eyebrow-raising detail in his "techno-optimist" vision of the future. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8jj4rw3xzpkhy0wt9ekqm))
- In a recent [tweet](https://x.com/pmarca/status/1882993091784880557), the American billionaire investor casually proclaimed that AI must "crash" everyone's wages before it can deliver us an economic utopia — one that'll definitely happen, and certainly not create a permanent underclass of have-nots. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8jkxth23dmw95scahahny))
- "A world in which human wages crash from AI — logically, necessarily — is a world in which productivity growth goes through the roof, and prices for goods and services crash to near zero," Andreessen wrote. "Consumer cornucopia. Everything you need and want for pennies." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8jtafkbbx8ddfjr1gdaap))
- Andreessen's tweet is a revealing example of the ruthless economic logic that underlies tech moguls' utopic visions of the future, in which progress is a foregone conclusion, rendering everyone's economic suffering in the interim merely a means to an end. Like overzealous fitness instructors, they always choose to [emphasize the need for pain](https://futurism.com/the-byte/sam-altman-warns-ai-destroy-jobs) to achieve anything. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8k67mb3rm5hy5kv6xap5z))
- The author of "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto," Andreessen also embodies how these brutal economic paradigm shifts are dressed up in benign rhetoric. AI-induced wage collapse is a consequence that happens "logically" and "necessarily," according to the billionaire. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8kmq1egsnac4d7htacvxw))
- Somehow, none of these AI evangelists' "optimistic" visions involve immediately improving people's lives in a meaningful way, or foreground measures to mitigate the tech's massively [disruptive potential to the job market](https://futurism.com/the-byte/sam-altman-ai-destroying-jobs), except perhaps with [broad gestures to a universal basic income](https://www.cnn.com/2024/05/23/tech/elon-musk-ai-your-job/index.html) — an idea that Andreessen, ever the unapologetic capitalist, [happens to hate](https://pmarca.substack.com/p/followup-to-why-ai-wont-cause-unemployment). (Per his manifesto, it would turn us all into "zoo animals.") ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8kvqm5meeqkfcnv2n2n5z))
- Larry Ellison for instance, cofounder of the software outfit Oracle, drooled about how [AI would supercharge the surveillance state](https://futurism.com/the-byte/billionaire-constant-ai-surveillance), ensuring that "citizens will be on their best behavior." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8m4e68zex0ev4y4sdp315))
- When asked in an interview about AI killing creative jobs, OpenAI's former chief technology officer Mira Murati [glibly suggested](https://futurism.com/the-byte/openai-exec-ai-creative-jobs-shouldnt-exist) that those jobs "shouldn't have been there in the first place." ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jjq8mbqb91z1qynntfpcft0m))
