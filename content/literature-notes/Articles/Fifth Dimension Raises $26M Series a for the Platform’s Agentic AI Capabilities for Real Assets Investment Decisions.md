## Highlights
- Fifth Dimension connects to a firm’s existing systems, including Yardi, Dealpath and SharePoint, with no data migration required. The platform deploys in two weeks and immediately begins unifying structured data and unstructured documents into a single intelligence layer. From there, Ellie, Fifth Dimension’s agentic AI, prepares analysis, screens deals, drafts IC memos, monitors portfolio variance, and surfaces risk proactively. Every conclusion is sourced, cited and audit-defensible. Institutional knowledge compounds over time, inherited by new starters. 
  Dr. Kate Jarvis, CEO & Co-founder, Fifth Dimension, said, “We are building the intelligence layer that this industry has never had. Firms that move now will define competitive advantage for the next decade.” 
  Lina Chong, Partner, HV Capital, said, “Real assets are the world’s largest, most complex asset class, yet the tech stack behind it hasn’t kept up. Fifth Dimension is building the layer operators have been missing, with the guardrails institutional capital requires.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krwxqdazpfc3a2fej1rpyw1z))
