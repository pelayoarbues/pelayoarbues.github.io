## Highlights
- My post on [o3 guessing locations from photos](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Apr/26/o3-photo-locations/) made it [to Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43803243) and by far the most interesting comments are from SamPatt, a self-described competitive [GeoGuessr](https://www.geoguessr.com/) player. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jsyc7b8vt52qyyxgcnf9sq5w))
- The photography matters a great deal - they're categorized into "Generations" of coverage. Gen 2 is low resolution, Gen 3 is pretty good but has a distinct car blur, Gen 4 is highest quality. Each country tends to have only one or two categories of coverage, and some are so distinct you can immediately know a location based solely on that (India is the best example here). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jsyc7hwr1pjan8475zjqydwt))
- Nigeria and Tunisia have follow cars. Senegal, Montenegro and Albania have large rifts in the sky where the panorama stitching software did a poor job. Some parts of Russia had recent forest fires and are very smokey. One road in Turkey is in absurdly thick fog. The list is endless, which is why it's so fun! ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jsyc7y1wzr3df428y471pqyv))
